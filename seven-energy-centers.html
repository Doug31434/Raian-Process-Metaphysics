Doug's definition

An energy center is the unique crystalline energetic shape the vortex creates, given frequency, laminarity and balance. The centers build on one another and are distinct at quantized intervals of angular velocity. Imaginally, they are seven chambers of one heart.

The three primary activities

Ra Asked why the red, yellow and blue centers are called primary, and whether they can be traced back to intelligent infinity: The red, yellow, and blue rays are primary because they signify activity of a primary nature. Ra closes the same answer: …if you consider the function of the Logos as representative of the Infinite Creator in effectuating the knowing of the Creator by the Creator, you may perhaps see the steps by which this may be accomplished (41.25).

Doug I read these three primary activities as universal and cosmological. They exist as one unified dynamic, and without them no experience would be possible. They build upon one another, and together they act as a metaphysical skeleton, a framework, on which the other processes act as flesh, ligaments and muscle.
