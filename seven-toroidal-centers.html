About this model

What this shows

I see each energy center as a toroidal field — a living vortex of light/love flowing up its core, out over the top, down the outside and back in, while the whole of it turns around the body's axis. And I see the centers two ways at once: as seats of conscious self-awareness, and as nodal points in a framework, the places where the larger pattern of the field crosses and gathers.

The seven are drawn as in the All Seven scene of my page The Seven Energy Centers Informed by the Ra Contact. Each center sits in its own flow membrane, which receives light from the center below and passes it to the one above, and intelligent energy rises along the axis from white below the root to white-gold above the crown (the white-gold is my proposal). The forms: the spoked wheel as a sphere of seventy-two spokes, orange as the three-petaled triquetra, yellow as nested faceted stars, green as the open lotus with its orb and its own toroidal field, blue as the many-faceted gem, indigo as the triquetra in the ajna arrangement, and violet as a thousand petals in four crowns. I don't think the centers literally look like these things. They are my renderings of the shapes Ra describes.

What Ra says
"The negative pole is the south pole, or the lower pole. The north, or upper pole, is positive. The crisscrossings of these spiraling energies form primary, secondary, and tertiary energy centers." 49.5
"The most important concept to grasp about the energy field is that the lower, or negative pole, will draw the universal energy into itself from the cosmos. Therefrom it will move upward to be met and reacted to by the positive spiraling energy moving downward from within." 49.5
"Meanwhile the Creator lies within. In the north pole the crown is already upon the head and the entity is potentially a god." 49.6
"As the entity develops the higher energy centers, however, these centers will then begin to express their nature by forming crystal structures." 51.7

~Ra describes the red center as often a spoked wheel, orange as a flower of three petals, yellow as a rounded, many-faceted star, green as the lotus, blue with perhaps a hundred facets and great flashing brilliance, indigo as a quieter triangular or three-petaled form, and violet as thousand-petaled 51.8.

Where the picture bends

The gold and silver streams are drawn as two strands crossing at each center, following Ra's image of interweaving spirals. Ra doesn't give their geometry, so the braid is illustrative. The toroidal fields, the seats, and the 3-D forms are mine.

The forms are my renderings, not portraits of any one person's centers. Ra says no two are alike, "just as in your world no two snowflakes are alike. However, each is regular" 51.8.

I'm offering these thoughts for what they are worth.
