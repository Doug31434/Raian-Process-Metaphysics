Build guide · 2 ft end-grain target

Knife Target Build

49 blocks of 4x4 stand on end inside a 2x6 frame, with a plywood back and a screw into every block. Drag the model to turn it, pull the parts apart, and step through the build.

    Frame screws (3 in) are not drawn. A real 4x4 measures 3.5 in, so seven blocks make a face 24.5 in square.

    Materials and tools

    Materials

    • 4 boards, 4x4 SPF or whitewood, 8 ft (skip Southern Yellow Pine and Douglas fir, which are harder and louder)
    • 1 board, 2x6 SPF, 10 ft
    • 1 sheet, 3/4 in exterior plywood, cut to 27.5 × 27.5 in
    • Titebond III waterproof wood glue, 1 large bottle
    • 49 exterior screws, #10 × 4 in, plus a few spares
    • 12 to 16 exterior screws, 3 in, for the frame
    • 1 ratchet strap or 2 bar clamps

    Tools

    • A saw that cuts a 4x4 (miter saw, or circular saw cutting from two sides)
    • Drill, with 1/8 in and 7/32 in bits
    • Tape measure, square, rubber mallet
    • Eye protection
    Safety

    Wear eye protection when cutting and driving screws. Keep people and pets behind the thrower, and check the face regularly for loose blocks.
