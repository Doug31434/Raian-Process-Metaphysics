Blanket Chest Ottoman

48 × 24 in, pine sides, plywood lid with a padded top, cedar-lined inside. Drag to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap any board.

Boards and their sizes

PartQtySize (in)Cut from

Ends are 22¼ in with rabbet joints. With plain butt joints (glue and screws), cut them 21½ in. Measure the inside of the assembled box before cutting the bottom.

One 20 × 72 in pine panel

Buy two panels: one gives the front and one end, the other gives the back and the other end. A third panel is a cheap spare.

Shopping list

  • 2 edge-glued pine panels, ¾ × 20 in × 6 ft
  • 1 plywood project panel, ¾ in × 2 × 4 ft (lid)
  • 1 plywood project panel, ½ in × 2 × 4 ft (bottom)
  • 2 select pine 1×2s, 8 ft (cleats)
  • 2 boxes aromatic cedar closet liner
  • Piano hinge 1½ × 48 in, 2 gas struts
  • 2 in upholstery foam, batting, 2 yd faux leather

The wood box stands 15¾ in. The finished height depends on the foam you pick after measuring the sofa seat.
