Seven Centers, Two Witnesses

The forms of the energy centers as the Indian tradition draws them, set beside Ra's description of their crystalline structures. Listed from the root to the crown.

"Each of the energy centers of the physical complex may be seen to have a distinctive crystalline structure in the more developed entity. Each will be somewhat different, just as in your world no two snowflakes are alike. However, each is regular."Ra, 51.8

Sources. Ra: The Ra Contact, Vol. 1, 51.8 (exact words). Tradition: the classical petal counts and inner figures of the six-chakra system (the Ṣaṭcakra-nirūpaṇa, translated in Arthur Avalon's The Serpent Power, 1919); modern drawings vary. The figures here are simple diagrams, not reproductions.

A caution. The traditional petals carry the 50 letters of the Sanskrit alphabet (4 + 6 + 10 + 12 + 16 + 2 = 50), so the counts are partly linguistic. Ra hedges every form ("often," "sometimes," "in many," "perhaps") and says the first three centers express mainly through speed of rotation (51.7).
