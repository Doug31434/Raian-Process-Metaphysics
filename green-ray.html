What You're Seeing

The column holds the lower triad (red, orange and yellow) and, above them, the green energy center drawn as our renders draw it: an onion-shaped field graded yellow-green at the root to green-blue at the neck, a pearly center, and the lotus around it. Moving points carry the flow; there are no arrows.

What Ra Says

Yellow ray "concerns the entity in relation to… groups, societies," and "is at the heart of bellicose actions" (32.2). Ra speaks of the energy centers' "lotus-shape" (51.8) and of green as "the great turning point," after which it "may then be turned outward, the entity then giving rather than receiving" (32.5). Invoking the higher self makes "a bridge betwixt space/time and time/space" (75.36).

What I Propose

My proposal: the Great BASH (Bellicose Attitude, Aggressive Actions, Scarred and Scared, Hope through Hostility) works as a vortex enmeshed with the lower triad, and Yehoshua (Ra's edition spells it Jehoshua, 33.11), by will and faith, became the collective human green-ray center, where humanity's field and the Solar Logos's field interpenetrate. In Stage 4 the light spreading into its spectrum at the King's Chamber draws the Living Ankh on the blue center's axis. In Stage 5 the ankh settles as the Christic Complex: its nexus on the yellow center, its circle around the heart. Stage 6 comes close to that nexus: a golden Star of David with the Sacred Heart of Jesus at its center, sculpted from the traditional iconography.

Where the Picture Bends

Ra doesn't describe the energy centers' fields, their colors of flow, or any vortex shape for the Great BASH. The tori, the onion and the vortex are my way of making a reading visible. The proportions are chosen for clarity, and the two fields at the heart are drawn as two shapes so that each stays legible. The heart sits at the Queen's Chamber position and the blue center at the King's Chamber position, spaced by their heights in the Great Pyramid. One more honest limit: when Don asked about a book linking the ankh shape to resonance in the pyramid, Ra called it "working with crayons" and said such shapes code mathematical relationships (55.14). The ankh here draws on Ra's other reading of it, as "an image of the eternal in and through manifestation" (93.24), and is my proposal.

I'm offering these thoughts for what they are worth.
