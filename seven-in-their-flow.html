The Seven in Their Flow

Each center caught as a still shot inside its outer flow: an onion of fine lines rising south to north, received from the center below and passed on to the one above.

  1. Energy center 1 · the root

    Red

    “The red energy center often is in the shape of the spoked wheel” (51.8)

  2. Energy center 2 · the movement

    Orange

    “The orange energy center in the flower shape containing three petals” (51.8)

  3. Energy center 3 · the stepping stone

    Yellow

    “The yellow center again in a rounded shape, many faceted, as a star” (51.8)

  4. Energy center 4 · the center ray

    Green

    “The green energy center sometimes called the lotus-shape, the number of points of crystalline structure dependent upon the strength of this center” (51.8)

  5. Energy center 5 · the first radiation

    Blue

    “The blue energy center capable of having perhaps one hundred facets and capable of great flashing brilliance” (51.8)

  6. Energy center 6 · the gateway

    Indigo

    “The indigo center a more quiet center which has the basic triangular, or three-petaled, shape in many, although some adepts who have balanced the lower energies may create more faceted forms” (51.8)

  7. Energy center 7 · the register

    Violet

    “The violet energy center is the least variable and is sometimes described in your philosophy as thousand-petaled, as it is the sum of the mind/body/spirit complex distortion totality” (51.8)

Ra's words are from 51.8, verified in the L/L edition. The forms are drawn from Ra's brief descriptions under Doug's direction: red, yellow, blue and violet are the canvas renders of November 3; orange and indigo are the knot renders of November 7; green is the new rendering of November 7, after Zone 4 of the poloidal core, with its own inner circulation and a pearly silver center. The outer flow membrane is Doug's reading: every center a toroidal field, coloured by what it receives from below and passes on above. Below red, intelligent energy, which is everywhere, rises white as a whole field of soft waves, unfocused yet moving upward, and is gathered into the root (after 54.27: the universal light enters "undifferentiated until it begins its filtering process through the energy centers"; and 59.11: "all light, or prana, is upward spiraling"). Red's wheel now has 72 spokes (November 7). Above violet, the white-gold column is Claude's proposal; Ra gives no colour for what leaves violet. Working chart, not for publication.
