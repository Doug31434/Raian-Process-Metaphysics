Two Triangles, One Star

Orange and indigo, the two three-petaled centers, set on either side of the heart. One points up, one points down; together they make the star that sits at green.

Energy center 4 · the center ray

✡Green

Lay orange's upward triangle and indigo's downward triangle over the heart and they make the six-pointed star. This is the figure Doug has already placed at green: one triangle rising, one descending, the exchange of the heart, the merkaba seen flat. Drawn here as an idealized star.

“The strong red/orange/yellow triad springboards the entity into the center ray of green” (39.10)

Ra · indigo at the brow

  • “Those feeling it within the forehead between the brows are experiencing indigo ray and so forth” (50.12).
  • A feeling at the indigo center is “instreamings at that energy center to be used either for the unblocking of this center, for its tuning to match the harmonics of its other energy centers, or to activate the gateway to intelligent infinity” (54.32).
  • Jim's question, read by Don, described pleasure and pressure in the frontal lobes that “feels like an orgasm in my brain.” Ra placed it at “the opening of the gateway, or indigo, mind complex” (49.2).
  • The frontal lobes' primary condition “is joy, or love, in its creative sense… This is the area tapped by the adept” (61.13).

Follows closely

  • Orange and indigo are the only two centers Ra gives three petals (51.8), and they stand at equal distance on either side of green, the middle of the seven.
  • Ra locates indigo at the brow (50.12) and names tuning as one of the three things a felt indigo instreaming may be doing (54.32).

Doug's reading

  • Orange's middle petal points up and indigo's points down, so lines joining their tips would make an upward and a downward triangle.
  • The traditional ajna image may carry Ra's “triangular, or three-petaled” in one figure: a triangle inside, two side petals, and the round center as a larger middle petal.
  • His own experience of indigo activation: inner vision centered on the eye, and pressure at the forehead.

Claude's proposal

  • The two triangles, laid together over the heart, make the six-pointed star Doug already places at green (41.25, 49.5, Zone 4). Orange rising and indigo descending would then be two halves of the heart's own figure.
  • Ra never names the triangles' directions, the star or the merkaba. The witness tradition draws ajna as two petals with a downward triangle; counting the center as a third petal is Doug's reading.
